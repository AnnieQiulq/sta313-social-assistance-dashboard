Data & interpretation

Source: Ontario Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services (Ontario Works and ODSP quarterly CMA caseload characteristics) and Statistics Canada (annual CMA population, 2021 CMA geography). Annual caseloads are averages of four quarterly observations; rates are per 1,000 residents. The selected-period map and ranking show percentage change in rates, not changes in the number of cases. The historical line chart always covers 2005–2025 so selected intervals can be read in longer-term context.

Coverage is limited to 15 Ontario CMAs, not rural Ontario. Ottawa–Gatineau uses its Ontario portion only. Case counts are not unique annual recipients. OW and ODSP duration categories differ; comparisons across programs should be interpreted cautiously. Patterns are descriptive and do not establish policy causation. Future expansion could include rural areas with compatible geographic data and jointly classified demographic counts; separate demographic totals cannot establish intersections between age and household type.
